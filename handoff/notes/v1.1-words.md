# v1.1 Fewer words

The user's top complaint was that players do not read the text. This pass cut words, not features: every
change is a string, in the data files, in the back-end messages and in a few UI labels. No data field was
added or renamed, no layout moved, and the story beats, `SOURCES.md` and `SI_USE.md` were left alone.

Events are now a 2 to 4 word title, one line of at most 15 words, and 1 to 3 word choices; the cost or odds chip

## What did not reach the target, and why

The target was a third off every screen and half off events and the debrief. Events reach it as text (55% off
the event block, 46% per event in the data), the debrief paragraphs reach it (63%), but the whole screens do not:
a debrief is mostly the score ledger, the money story, buttons and the story lines. Getting further needs a layout
decision (fold the real-Earth box and the closest-mission card behind a tap, merge the part bins' "Needed" and
"Why" labels), which is outside a strings-only pass. See `needs_decision` in the hand-back.

## Changed in the code (not only strings)

- `js/ui-mission.js`: the duplicate "Reach the science target for the third star." line is gone (the next-star
  line says it); the closest-mission card lists one shared trait, not all; the choice chip says "Science x0.6"
  and "Protected" (it used to add "from now on" and "no effect").
- Strings only in `js/game.js` (`runChecks` messages), `js/mission.js` (`limitingFactor` advice, `realWorldSummary`,
  `withLiveNow`), `js/ui-design.js` (hint, "go to" and "do" lines) and the data files.
